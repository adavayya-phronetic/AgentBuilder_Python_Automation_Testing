FAQs

Everything you need to know about the Developer Platform and building AI agents.

What is the Developer Platform (Agent Builder)?

The Developer Platform by Phronetic AI enables you to build, configure, and deploy AI agents tailored to your workflow. It provides modular components, plugin support, and tools to connect data sources and external APIs.

Who is the Developer Platform built for?

The platform is designed for developers, AI engineers, and technical teams building production-ready AI agents. It supports both rapid prototyping and enterprise-grade deployments across internal tools, customer workflows, and operational systems.

What types of AI agents can I build?

You can build task-based, workflow-driven, and autonomous agents that interact with APIs, documents, databases, and real-time systems. Common use cases include knowledge agents, operations agents, support automation, and domain-specific AI assistants.

Does the platform support custom models and external APIs?

Yes. The platform supports integration with custom models, proprietary APIs, and third-party services. Developers can connect agents to internal data sources, external tools, and cloud services to create end-to-end workflows.

How does deployment and scaling work?

Agents can be deployed in controlled environments and scaled based on usage requirements. The platform supports versioning, updates, and managed rollouts to ensure reliability as agents move from development to production.

How secure is the Developer Platform?

Security is built into the platform architecture. Data access is controlled through permissions and environment isolation, with configurable deployment options to meet enterprise security and compliance requirements.
